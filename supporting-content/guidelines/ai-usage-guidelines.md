# AI Usage Guidelines

## AI Tool Output Ownership

The [AI Usage Guidelines](../../guidelines/contributors/ai-usage-guidelines.md) require contributors to ensure that the terms of their AI tools do not conflict with FOCUS licensing. This aligns with the [Linux Foundation Generative AI Policy](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/generative-ai).

The following summarizes how major AI coding tools handle output ownership as of January 2025. Contributors should verify current terms before use, as these may change.

### OpenAI (ChatGPT, Codex)

OpenAI assigns output ownership to users:

> As between you and OpenAI, and to the extent permitted by applicable law, you (a) retain your ownership rights in Input and (b) own the Output. We hereby assign to you all our right, title, and interest, if any, in and to Output.

Users may not represent that Output was human-generated when it was not, and may not use Output to develop competing models.

Source: [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/)

### Google (Gemini)

Google does not claim ownership of generated content. For Google Workspace, generated output is considered Customer Data and Google does not assert ownership rights in new intellectual property created in the output.

Users grant Google a license to use submitted content and generated responses. Free-tier usage data may be used for model training; paid tiers offer opt-out.

Sources: [Generative AI Additional Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai), [Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)

### Anthropic (Claude)

Anthropic assigns output ownership to users under both consumer and commercial terms:

Consumer terms:

> Subject to your compliance with our Terms, we assign to you all of our right, title, and interest -- if any -- in Outputs.

Commercial/API terms:

> Anthropic agrees that Customer (a) retains all rights to its Inputs, and (b) owns its Outputs. Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services.

Sources: [Consumer Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms), [Commercial Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms)

### GitHub Copilot

GitHub does not own Suggestions. Users retain ownership of their Code, including Suggestions they choose to include. Business and Enterprise customers receive IP indemnification.

Free-tier data may be used for model training (configurable in settings). GitHub acknowledges that whether AI-generated suggestions are legally "ownable" depends on jurisdiction.

Sources: [GitHub Copilot Product Specific Terms](https://github.com/customer-terms/github-copilot-product-specific-terms), [GitHub Terms for Additional Products](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-for-additional-products-and-features)

## Summary

None of the major AI coding tools place contractual restrictions on their output that conflict with FOCUS' [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license or [intellectual property policies](https://github.com/FinOps-Open-Cost-and-Usage-Spec/foundation/blob/main/operating_procedures.md#514-consistency--ipr-reviews). All four vendors either assign output rights to users or explicitly disclaim ownership.
