# Commitment Pricing

[*Commitment discounts*](#glossary:commitment-discount) appear in a [*price list*](#glossary:price-list) as commitment purchase SKU Price records. [ChargeCategory](#datamodel.skuprice.chargecategory) identifies them as "Purchase", and it governs which of the purchase-describing columns carry values. [CommitmentApplicability](#datamodel.skuprice.commitmentapplicability) on each purchase record states which usage the *commitment discount* covers and how.

Aura Web offers two commitment instruments, each under three payment models. A reservation commits to a specific resource, the standard virtual machine, and is bought for a one-year term. A flexible spend plan commits to an amount of spend rather than to a resource, and is bought at face value, one unit of price for each unit of committed spend. The on-demand rate for the standard machine is 0.384000 per hour.

## Commitment Purchases

[**CSV Example**](/specification/data/sku_price_examples/sku_price_commitment_pricing.csv)

Note the following details in the example dataset:

* All six commitment purchase records have a ChargeCategory of "Purchase". [PurchaseDurationType](#datamodel.skuprice.purchasedurationtype) is "1 Year", and [PurchasePaymentModel](#datamodel.skuprice.purchasepaymentmodel) identifies the payment model.
* Each payment model is a distinct price offering and is therefore represented by a separate record with its own [SkuPriceId](#datamodel.skuprice.skupriceid), even when the records share the same [UnitPrice](#datamodel.skuprice.unitprice). A [*service provider*](#glossary:service-provider) may set different UnitPrice values for different payment models, or may charge the same UnitPrice across payment models. The reservation and the spend plan in this example illustrate these two patterns.
* The reservation purchase prices are 1893.600000, 1998.800000, and 2104.000000 for "All Upfront", "Partial Upfront", and "No Upfront", respectively. [PricingUnit](#datamodel.skuprice.pricingunit) is "Units", so UnitPrice represents the price of one reservation, and PurchaseDurationType specifies the one-year term. The payment model affects the UnitPrice.
* The spend plan purchase price is 1.000000 for all three payment models. The plan is purchased per unit of committed spend, so UnitPrice represents the price of one unit of commitment. The payment model does not affect the UnitPrice, so the three records share the same UnitPrice while remaining separate price offerings. PricingUnit is "USD", representing the unit in which the commitment is measured, and [PricingCurrency](#datamodel.skuprice.pricingcurrency) is also "USD", representing the currency in which the purchase is priced. These columns describe different aspects of the price: the unit of the commitment and the currency of the price.
* The two commitment instruments have different [SkuId](#datamodel.skuprice.skuid) values because they represent different products. The reservation records share "AURAWEB-COMPUTE-RESERVATION-VM-STD", while the spend plan records share "AURAWEB-COMMITMENT-FLEXSPEND". [CommitmentDiscountCategory](#datamodel.skuprice.commitmentdiscountcategory) also separates them: "Usage" on the reservation records, which commit to a resource, and "Spend" on the spend plan records, which commit to an amount of spend.
* CommitmentApplicability on the three reservation records covers the standard virtual machine (SkuId) with a Factor of 1.0, so one reservation covers one hour of that machine. Spread over the 8,760 hours of the one-year term, the reservation prices give 0.216164, 0.228174, and 0.240183 per hour for "All Upfront", "Partial Upfront", and "No Upfront", respectively, against the on-demand rate of 0.384000.
* CommitmentApplicability on the three spend plan records covers all usage priced under the "Aura Web Compute" service (PricingServiceName) at 28 percent off its unit price. A covered hour of the standard virtual machine costs 0.276480 at the list unit price of 0.384000, and 0.235008 at the contracted unit price of 0.326400, and consumes the same amount of the commitment, which is measured in "USD".
* The extract also includes the on-demand usage record for the standard virtual machine, for comparison. It has a ChargeCategory of "Usage", null PurchaseDurationType and PurchasePaymentModel, and a null CommitmentApplicability. These columns describe the purchase of a *commitment discount* and therefore do not apply to usage records. Usage covered by a *commitment discount* needs no record of its own: its unit price follows from the purchase record of the *commitment discount* that covers it.
* When a particular consumption record is covered by a commitment, this is reflected in the [EffectiveCost](#datamodel.costandusage.effectivecost) and [BilledCost](#datamodel.costandusage.billedcost) columns in the [Cost and Usage](#datamodel.costandusage) dataset, but not in the [ListCost](#datamodel.costandusage.listcost), [ContractedCost](#datamodel.costandusage.contractedcost), [ContractedUnitPrice](#datamodel.costandusage.contractedunitprice), or [ListUnitPrice](#datamodel.costandusage.listunitprice) columns.
