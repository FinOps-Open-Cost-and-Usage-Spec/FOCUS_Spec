# Includes Billing and Payment Currency Differences

The [Includes Billing and Payment Currency Differences](#omc) operating model condition represents a verifiable state indicating whether the [operating model](#term) includes billing and payment in different currencies.

## Requirements

[IncludesBillingPaymentCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST adhere to the following requirements:

* [IncludesBillingPaymentCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST evaluate to true when the [operating model](#term) includes billing and payment in different currencies.
* [IncludesBillingPaymentCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST evaluate to false when the [operating model](#term) does not include billing and payment in different currencies.

## Operating Model Condition ID

IncludesBillingPaymentCurrencyDifferences

## Display Name

Includes Billing and Payment Currency Differences

## Description

A verifiable state indicating whether the [operating model](#term) includes billing and payment in different currencies.

## Version Introduced

1.5
