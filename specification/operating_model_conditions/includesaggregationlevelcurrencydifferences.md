# Includes Aggregation Level Currency Differences

The [Includes Aggregation Level Currency Differences](#omc) operating model condition represents a verifiable state indicating whether the [operating model](#term) includes the representation of [billing currency](#term) and payment currency at different aggregation levels on payable [invoices](#term).

## Requirements

[IncludesAggregationLevelCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST adhere to the following requirements:

* [IncludesAggregationLevelCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST evaluate to true when the [operating model](#term) includes the representation of [billing currency](#term) and payment currency at different aggregation levels on payable [invoices](#term).
* [IncludesAggregationLevelCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST evaluate to false when the [operating model](#term) does not include the representation of [billing currency](#term) and payment currency at different aggregation levels on payable [invoices](#term).

## Operating Model Condition ID

IncludesAggregationLevelCurrencyDifferences

## Display Name

Includes Aggregation Level Currency Differences

## Description

A verifiable state indicating whether the [operating model](#term) includes the representation of [billing currency](#term) and payment currency at different aggregation levels on payable [invoices](#term).
