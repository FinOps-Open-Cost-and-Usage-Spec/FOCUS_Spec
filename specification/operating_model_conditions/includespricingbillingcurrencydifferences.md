# Includes Pricing-Billing Currency Differences

The [Includes Pricing-Billing Currency Differences](#omc) operating model condition represents a verifiable state indicating whether the [operating model](#term) includes pricing and billing in different currencies.

## Requirements

[IncludesPricingBillingCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST adhere to the following requirements:

* [IncludesPricingBillingCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST evaluate to true when the [operating model](#term) includes pricing and billing in different currencies.
* [IncludesPricingBillingCurrencyDifferences](#omc) MUST evaluate to false when the [operating model](#term) does not include pricing and billing in different currencies.

## Operating Model Condition ID

IncludesPricingBillingCurrencyDifferences

## Display Name

Includes Pricing-Billing Currency Differences

## Description

A verifiable state indicating whether the [operating model](#term) includes pricing and billing in different currencies.

## Version Introduced

1.5
