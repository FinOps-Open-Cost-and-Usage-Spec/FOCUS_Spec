# Includes Identity Attribution

The Includes Identity Attribution condition represents a verifiable state indicating whether the [*operating model*](#glossary:operating-model) includes usage or purchase of [*resources*](#glossary:resource) or [*services*](#glossary:service) initiated by a [*principal*](#glossary:principal).

## Requirements

IncludesIdentityAttribution MUST adhere to the following requirements:

* IncludesIdentityAttribution MUST evaluate to true when the *operating model* includes attributing usage or purchase of *resources* or *services* to *principals*.
* IncludesIdentityAttribution MUST evaluate to false when the *operating model* does not include attributing usage or purchase of *resources* or *services* to *principals*.

## Condition ID

IncludesIdentityAttribution

## Display Name

Includes Identity Attribution

## Description

A verifiable state indicating whether the *operating model* includes attributing usage or purchase of *resources* or *services* to *principals*.
