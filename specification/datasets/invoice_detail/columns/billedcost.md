# Billed Cost

Billed Cost represents the cost of a [*charge*](#glossary:charge) as invoiced by the [invoice issuer](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoiceissuername) in a given [*billing period*](#glossary:billing-period).

For all *charges*, Billed Cost reflects all applicable pricing adjustments (e.g., reduced pricing from [*negotiated discounts*](#glossary:negotiated-discount) or [*commitment discounts*](#glossary:commitment-discount)). For purchase *charges*, Billed Cost includes any portion invoiced in the given *billing period*. For usage *charges*, Billed Cost excludes any portion covered by related purchase *charges* (e.g., *commitments*, pre-payments, or marketplace purchases), regardless of when those related charges are invoiced.

Billed Cost is denominated in the [Billing Currency](#datasets.invoicedetail.billingcurrency). Billed Cost is commonly used to support FinOps activities, including invoice reconciliation, [*cash-flow-based*](#glossary:cash-based-accounting) forecasting, budgeting, and cost allocation.

BilledCost adheres to the following requirements:

* BilledCost MUST be of type Decimal.
* BilledCost MUST conform to [NumericFormat](#attributes.numericformat) requirements.
* BilledCost MUST NOT be null.
* BilledCost MUST be a valid decimal value.
* BilledCost MUST be 0 for *charges* where payments are received by a third party (e.g., marketplace transactions).
* BilledCost MUST be denominated in the BillingCurrency.
* The sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoiceid) MUST match the sum of the payable amount provided in the corresponding invoice with the same ID generated by the [InvoiceIssuerName](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoiceissuername).
* The sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceDetailId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoicedetailid) MUST match the sum of the payable amount provided in the corresponding invoice.
* When comparing the sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoiceid) across the [InvoiceDetail](#datasets.invoicedetail) and [CostAndUsage](#datasets.costandusage) datasets, BilledCost adheres to the following requirements:
  * The sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoiceid) in [InvoiceDetail](#datasets.invoicedetail) MAY differ from the sum of [BilledCost](#datasets.costandusage.billedcost) that same [InvoiceId](#datasets.costandusage.invoiceid) in [CostAndUsage](#datasets.costandusage) when [ChargeCategory](#datasets.invoicedetail.chargecategory) = "Tax".
  * Otherwise, the sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoiceid) in [InvoiceDetail](#datasets.invoicedetail) MUST match the sum of [BilledCost](#datasets.costandusage.billedcost) that same [InvoiceId](#datasets.costandusage.invoiceid) in [CostAndUsage](#datasets.costandusage).
* When comparing the sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceDetailId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoicedetailid) across the [InvoiceDetail](#datasets.invoicedetail) and [CostAndUsage](#datasets.costandusage) datasets, BilledCost adheres to the following requirements:
  * The sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceDetailId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoicedetailid) in [InvoiceDetail](#datasets.invoicedetail) MAY differ from the sum of [BilledCost](#datasets.costandusage.billedcost) that same [InvoiceDetailId](#datasets.costandusage.invoicedetailid) in [CostAndUsage](#datasets.costandusage) when [ChargeCategory](#datasets.invoicedetail.chargecategory) = "Tax".
  * Otherwise, the sum of BilledCost for a given [InvoiceDetailId](#datasets.invoicedetail.invoicedetailid) in [InvoiceDetail](#datasets.invoicedetail) MUST match the sum of [BilledCost](#datasets.costandusage.billedcost) that same [InvoiceDetailId](#datasets.costandusage.invoicedetailid) in [CostAndUsage](#datasets.costandusage).
