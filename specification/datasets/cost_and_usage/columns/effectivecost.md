# Effective Cost

[Effective Cost](#col) represents the cost of a [charge](#term) based on the [resources](#term) used, [services](#term) used, or [contract commitments](#term) recognized in a given [charge period](#term). [Effective Cost](#col) differs from [Billed Cost](#col) when [covering charges](#term) (e.g., prepaid or postpaid commitment purchases) are recorded separately from the [covered charges](#term) to which they are applied.

For all [charges](#term), [Effective Cost](#col) reflects all applicable pricing adjustments (e.g., reduced pricing from [negotiated discounts](#term) or [commitment discounts](#term)). For usage [charges](#term), [Effective Cost](#col) includes the recognized portion of *[Billed Cost](#col)* from related purchase [charges](#term) (e.g., amortized portions of prepayments, drawdowns). For purchase [charges](#term), [Effective Cost](#col) excludes any amounts recognized in related usage [charges](#term) (e.g., usage [covered](#term) by [covering charges](#term) such as [commitments](#term), prepayments, or marketplace purchases which draw down based on usage), regardless of when those related [charges](#term) are invoiced.

[Effective Cost](#col) is denominated in the [Billing Currency](#col). [Effective Cost](#col) is commonly used to support FinOps activities, including [accrual-based](#term:accrual-based-accounting) reporting, forecasting, and cost allocation.

## Requirements

[EffectiveCost](#col) MUST adhere to the following requirements:

* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST be of type Decimal.
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST conform to [NumericFormat](#attr) requirements.
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST NOT be null.
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST be denominated in the [BillingCurrency](#col).
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST reflect all applicable pricing adjustments, including but not limited to [negotiated discounts](#term), [commitment discounts](#term), and other applicable discount programs.
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST equal [BilledCost](#col) when [ChargeCategory](#col) is ["Usage"](#val:CostAndUsage.ChargeCategory) and the [charge](#term) is not [covered](#term) by other eligible [charges](#term).
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST equal [BilledCost](#col) when [ChargeCategory](#col) is ["Purchase"](#val:CostAndUsage.ChargeCategory) and the [charge](#term) is neither intended to cover other eligible [charges](#term) nor [covered](#term) by other eligible [charges](#term).
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST equal [BilledCost](#col) when [ChargeCategory](#col) is ["Tax"](#val:CostAndUsage.ChargeCategory) or "Credit".
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MAY differ from [BilledCost](#col) when [ChargeCategory](#col) is ["Adjustment"](#val:CostAndUsage.ChargeCategory).
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST include any portion of the [BilledCost](#col) of *covering* purchase [charges](#term) (i.e., [ChargeCategory](#col) is ["Purchase"](#val:CostAndUsage.ChargeCategory)) that is applied to this [charge](#term).
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST be 0 when [ChargeCategory](#col) is ["Purchase"](#val:CostAndUsage.ChargeCategory) and the purchase is intended to cover related eligible [charges](#term). This requirement applies even when the [covered charges](#term) originate from different [CostAndUsage](#ds) [dataset instances](#term), possibly from a different [ServiceProviderName](#col).
* [EffectiveCost](#col) MUST be 0 for [charges](#term) generated by entities that do not originate the cost and usage data, to avoid double-counting when merging multiple [dataset instances](#term).
* The sum of [EffectiveCost](#col) across all related *covering* and [covered charges](#term) MUST equal the sum of [BilledCost](#col) across the same set of [charges](#term), within the [charge period](#term) of the [covering charges](#term), when both the *covering* and [covered charges](#term) are present in the [dataset instance](#term).
* The sum of [EffectiveCost](#col) for a given [billing period](#term) MAY differ from the sum of [BilledCost](#col) when [covered](#term) and [covering charges](#term) span multiple [billing periods](#term) or [billing accounts](#term), or when only one side of a covering relationship is present in the [dataset instance](#term).
